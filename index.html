The Hidden Cost of Data Centers

An open-source tracker showing the energy, water, carbon, and community costs behind AI and cloud infrastructure.

415 TWh

Estimated global data-center electricity consumption in 2024.

Why this matters

Data centers power AI, cloud tools, streaming, enterprise software, and modern digital life. But their hidden costs include electricity demand, water use, grid upgrades, emissions, and local community pressure.

What this repository will track

  • Energy use
  • Water use
  • Carbon emissions
  • Electricity price pressure
  • Local community impact
Open-source project. Data sources: IEA, DOE, EIA, academic research, and public reports.
